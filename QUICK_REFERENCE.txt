💡 WHY PHASE-ADAPT WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Masking (0-200 steps):  Protects exploration, prevents mode collapse
Zeroing (200-1000):     Sharpens optimization, drives correct solutions

Trade-off: Sacrifices easy tasks for hard task robustness
